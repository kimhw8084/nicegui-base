# Wave 74 — Enterprise Sustained-Operations Evidence Renewal & Operational Assurance Continuity

Wave 74 is additive over Waves 59–73. It independently reverifies Wave 73 incident/rollback audit archives, accepts hash-bound external renewal evidence, evaluates deterministic freshness/expiry windows, and produces operational-assurance continuity dossiers/packages without changing historical Wave 73 acceptance/audit truth.

Core states:
- renewal: `RENEWED`, `PENDING`, `BLOCKED`;
- freshness: `CURRENT`, `EXPIRING`, `EXPIRED`, `MISSING`, `CONTRADICTORY`;
- continuity: `ASSURED`, `EXPIRING`, `PENDING`, `BLOCKED`.

No continuous monitoring, incident response, rollback, deployment, publication, or company approval is performed by the generic framework.
